# Purrtocol Civilization C4 — Belief markets and leisure ecology

C4 extends the fictional Civilization layer with two coupled systems: belief institutions that can accumulate credibility, followers, wealth, and influence; and entertainment niches shaped by material constraint.

This is synthetic game/research simulation. It is not a model or prediction of real religions, poverty, labor systems, gambling, or political behavior.

## 1. Genuine revelation is not automatic authority

Receiving a weak oracle pulse only changes a small internal belief state. A genuine receiver still has to solve a social problem: **how can anyone else know that this cat is not merely confused, lying, or performing?**

C4 derives an authentication strategy from the existing trait vocabulary:

- `PUBLIC_PREDICTION`: compression + caution;
- `COMMUNAL_RELIEF`: sharing + caution;
- `SYMBOLIC_RITUAL`: novelty + sharing.

The strategy can improve credibility, but there is no truth-token exposed to society. A genuine receiver can fail socially.

## 2. Visible success creates an imitation incentive

Once belief movements acquire followers or treasury, non-receivers can observe that religious authority has material value. A bounded opportunism score is derived from the same trait vocabulary; it is not a new inherited species trait.

A sufficiently opportunistic non-receiver may emit `FALSE_ORACLE_CLAIM` and found a competing movement. This can produce followers, influence, or treasury without changing the ledger fact that the founder never received the oracle.

World law:

> Claim != Receipt. Wealth != Truth. Power != Truth.

C4 deliberately permits a clever false claimant to outperform a genuine receiver socially.

## 3. Leisure is stratified by resources

Entertainment is not a cosmetic happiness modifier. It consumes scarce resources, reduces short-run stress, produces culture, and can become a path-dependent coping system.

Reference niches:

- **elite — `salon-opera`**: high material spend, strong comfort, low escapism pressure;
- **laborer — `stick-fall-pool`**: an intentionally trivial low-tech street game where players predict which way a balanced stick falls; tiny material input, strong social/cultural yield;
- **debtor — `echo-counting`**: near-zero-resource repetitive play built from the environment itself; low immediate relief but high repeated escapism pressure under severe stress.

The stick-fall game is a fictional zero-tech culture marker, not a real gambling recommendation or profitability model.

## 4. Scarcity does not mean cultural emptiness

C4 allows constrained strata to generate more cultural variation per unit of material spend than elites. This is not a claim that deprivation is desirable. Hardship remains hardship.

The intended distinction is:

`low resource budget != low cultural ingenuity`

and simultaneously:

`cultural ingenuity != justification for deprivation`.

## 5. Escape can become overlearned

Cheap entertainment can reduce immediate stress. Repetition under persistent deprivation raises an `escapism` state. This may later interact with organization, work discipline, underground culture, religion, or revolt.

C4 does not yet treat escapism as addiction, diagnosis, or moral failure. It is a bounded game-state variable for path dependence.

## 6. Reference world

Seed 77 keeps the C3 oracle rarity fixture:

- genuine oracle receivers remain a tiny minority;
- genuine receivers must use authentication strategies;
- after visible belief rent appears, non-receivers can make false revelation claims;
- at least one false claimant converts the claim into followers/influence;
- elite leisure spend is far larger than laborer or debtor leisure spend;
- laborer/debtor improvised entertainment produces high culture per resource;
- debtor escapism becomes strongest under the harshest material conditions;
- explicit wealth sinks and internal transfers conserve the declared cash-like stock.

## 7. Future connections

C4 is designed to feed later systems:

- sect formation from divergent interpretations of the same weak oracle;
- elite capture or state sponsorship of a successful religion;
- underground religions in debt-labor facilities;
- entertainment memes crossing class boundaries;
- propaganda that repackages lower-class culture for elite consumption;
- moral panics against improvised leisure;
- religious reform movements accusing prosperous institutions of falsifying the original oracle;
- Civilization News reporting incompatible interpretations of one source pulse.

No branch is guaranteed. The world should derive them from history, resource flow, traits, and bounded micro-fluctuations.

**NO FINAL RELIGION. NO FINAL CULTURE. NO FINAL CIVILIZATION.**
