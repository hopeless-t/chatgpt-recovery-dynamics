# Purrtocol - the Recovery Cat

Purrtocol is the deliberately unnecessary research mascot for this repository.

## Character brief

A tiny cyan-and-green cat-shaped network operator wearing amber recovery goggles.

It watches many red retry packets approach, calmly merges them into one cyan observation packet, waits through provisional recovery, and only then carries one green snapshot home.

## Personality
- patient;
- suspicious of duplicate work;
- allergic to retry storms;
- obsessed with state transitions;
- refuses to call E "Healthy";
- says "Visualization != Evidence" at parties.

## Motto
> **Retry less. Observe more.**

## Canonical pose
Purrtocol sits on a router-like panel with:
- left ear marked B;
- amber goggles marked E;
- green tail-tip marked H;
- one small cyan packet in its paws.

## Why a cat?

No empirical reason whatsoever.

That statement has unusually high confidence.
