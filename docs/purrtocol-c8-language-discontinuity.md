# Purrtocol Civilization C8 — Language Discontinuity and Institutional Mistranslation

> **Text != meaning.**
>
> **Script detection != decipherment.**
>
> **Evidence does not guarantee correction.**

C8 extends the post-collapse archaeology model into language loss. A civilization may leave labels, manuals, numbers, arrows, warning placards, and archive fragments behind while the language, script conventions, units, grammar, technical vocabulary, and cultural context needed to read them disappear.

This is fictional simulation and game-system design. It is not a probability claim about any real historical culture.

## Why extinction breaks text

A surviving inscription does not carry its language decoder with it.

After a deep civilizational discontinuity, a successor culture may lack:

- script continuity,
- language continuity,
- shared pronunciation,
- grammar,
- negation markers,
- technical vocabulary,
- units and measurement conventions,
- archive literacy,
- the social context that made a label obvious,
- a bilingual key comparable to a Rosetta Stone.

Therefore the model separates three gates:

```text
marks are noticed
  -> marks are recognized as writing
  -> writing is deciphered
  -> deciphered words are placed in the correct historical context
```

Passing one gate does not imply passing the next.

## Reference corpus

The bounded reference bank exposes successor cultures to eight fragments from the advanced civilization:

- `OXYGEN`,
- `WARNING HOT SURFACE`,
- `DO NOT STEP`,
- `OPEN`,
- `MAX PRESSURE 300 KPA`,
- `FLIGHT OPERATIONS MANUAL`,
- `EMERGENCY EXIT`,
- `NO SMOKING`.

The historical ledger retains the original text for auditability. Successor cultures do not receive the original meaning.

## Expected Purrtocol mistakes

C8 allows mistakes such as:

- `OXYGEN` -> the ancestor **Oxy Gene**,
- `WARNING HOT SURFACE` -> **Warm Sun Blessing**,
- `DO NOT STEP` -> **Step Here Ritual**,
- `OPEN` -> **Prosperity Rune**,
- `MAX PRESSURE 300 KPA` -> **Sacred Number 300**,
- `FLIGHT OPERATIONS MANUAL` -> **Sky Liturgy**,
- `EMERGENCY EXIT` -> **Spear-God Shrine** or pilgrimage marker,
- `NO SMOKING` -> **Smoke Offering Zone**.

These are candidate interpretations selected from successor conditions; no single interpretation is canonical for every world.

## Negation failure

Negation is especially fragile because a culture must know that a small word or grammatical marker reverses the rest of the instruction.

A lost `NOT` can transform:

```text
DO NOT STEP
```

into a sacred stepping instruction.

Likewise a lost `NO` can transform a prohibition into a smoke-offering ritual.

This creates the world law:

**A surviving sentence can preserve every visible symbol and still invert its practical meaning.**

## Unit death

Numbers may survive while units do not.

`300 KPA` can therefore retain the visually salient number `300` while losing pressure, kilo-, pascal, and the concept of a safe operating envelope.

The number may become:

- a sacred count,
- an ancestor total,
- a tax ceiling,
- a ritual calendar marker.

A preserved numeral is not preserved engineering.

## Wrong translations can become institutions

C8 allows mistranslation to stabilize because useful or prestigious institutions may grow around it.

Examples include:

- an Oxy Gene ancestor cult,
- a Warm Sun purification order,
- a Sacred Step pilgrimage,
- a merchant prosperity-sigil network,
- a Three Hundred canon,
- an ascent-liturgy school,
- a pilgrimage direction-sign system,
- a smoke-offering guild.

Once an institution benefits from a translation, contradictory evidence may be absorbed as metaphor, heresy, dialect, or a secondary revelation instead of correcting the original claim.

## Scholars can still be interesting without being correct

Scholar Purrtocols are not useless. They can:

- notice repeated glyphs,
- compare sign placement,
- identify that some marks are probably writing,
- build competing translation schools,
- preserve fragments,
- detect numerical repetition,
- recover local functional hints from icons and placement.

But none of those abilities guarantee a correct translation.

A scholar can be methodologically sophisticated and still start from a corpus too small and a continuity gap too large to recover the original language.

## Deterministic balance contract

The reference bank uses 32 successor cultures over 8 textual relics, producing 256 interpretations.

The model requires:

- original language continuity to remain near zero,
- exact translation to remain extremely rare,
- many fragments not even to be recognized as writing,
- at least 24 distinct successor interpretations,
- at least three divergent readings for every textual relic,
- many confident but wrong translations,
- some wrong-but-locally-useful functional hints,
- multiple negation inversions,
- multiple unknown-unit sacralizations,
- established doctrine sometimes to absorb contradictory evidence rather than immediately self-correct.

This is a gameplay balance contract, not a real-world estimate.

## World laws

1. `Text != meaning`.
2. `Script detection != decipherment`.
3. `Decipherment != technical recovery`.
4. `Social confidence != translation accuracy`.
5. `Lost negation can invert a safety instruction`.
6. `Unknown unit can become sacred number`.
7. `Contradictory evidence can be absorbed into doctrine`.
8. `Same corpus can generate incompatible translation schools`.
9. `Post-extinction semantic reset is default`.
10. `No Rosetta Stone is guaranteed`.
11. `NO FINAL CIVILIZATION`.

The intended player reaction is:

> "They found the manual. Surely this fixes the misunderstanding."

followed by:

> "Why is the flight manual now the funeral hymnbook, and why does the engineering faculty call the literal translation a vulgar modernist forgery?"
