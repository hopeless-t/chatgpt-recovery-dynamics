**Purrtocol** is the deliberately unnecessary Recovery Cat.

Japanese nickname: **パープロトコル / パケにゃ**

## Motto

> **Retry less. Observe more.**

## Job description

- merge noisy duplicate triggers;
- carry tiny observation packets;
- wait through provisional recovery;
- refuse to call E Healthy;
- carry one snapshot only when stable;
- remind everyone that Visualization != Evidence.

No empirical reason for the cat has been established.
