# External Archaeology

Public Reddit / Hacker News reports are retained as historical weak evidence.

They are useful for:

- recurrence history
- symptom morphology
- model constraints
- replication targets

They are **not** treated as IID telemetry and do not establish a shared root cause.

The archive intentionally keeps provenance, incident context, possible overlap, and a negative control.

Full note: [external evidence archaeology](https://github.com/hopeless-t/chatgpt-recovery-dynamics/blob/main/docs/external-evidence.md)

## Project archaeology: The Trust-Level Paradox

The author also preserved a Community UI anecdote in which:

1. an iOS ChatGPT app feature-improvement suggestion was posted;
2. the community account was placed on temporary hold shortly afterward;
3. a later system message promoted the account's trust level and encouraged
   participation;
4. the same interface showed "Account temporarily on hold" in related messages.

This records chronology and interface state only. It does **not** establish that
the feature-improvement post caused the hold.
